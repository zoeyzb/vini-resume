# Portfolio motion & experience audit — 27 July 2026

Supersedes `AUDIT_2026-07-26.md`.

## What this pass set out to do

Raise the site from "clean and readable" to something a recruiter remembers, without
rebuilding the layout, the typography pairing, or the editorial voice — all of which were
working. The brief: particle-based motion, scroll choreography between sections, a premium
glossy surface treatment, a real atmospheric background, a more interactive guide pet, and
three animated story diagrams for the projects that have no live link.

## What was actually wrong

### Blocking, recruiter-facing
- **The résumé PDF was linked from nowhere.** `public/Sierra_Byrd_Resume.pdf` shipped with
  every build and no anchor pointed at it.
- **The nav reached 5 of 10 sections**, and its contact button was hidden below the `sm`
  breakpoint — so phones had no contact action at all.

### Visual identity
- **Two palettes were fighting.** The Tailwind theme block defined a violet system
  (`--color-accent`) used by case studies, project visuals, `FlowSteps` and `BrowserFrame`,
  while `:root` defined the actual coral/amber/mint/sky system. Opening a project changed
  the colour of the whole site.
- **Six accent hues** were live at once with no role assignment.
- **A full-bleed cream band** (`SideWork`) strobed against the black page and hid the fixed
  particle field for a whole screen.
- **Four competing numbering systems**: section chapters 1–9 (2 missing), transition labels
  01–04, a 6-item rail, and a 5-stop pet menu.

### Motion
- **Three uncoordinated scroll systems** — the rail, the per-section parallax and the
  particle field each ran their own scroll maths, so nothing landed together.
- **Several section crossings morphed a shape into itself.** `SECTION_FORMATION` reused the
  same formation for adjacent sections, so the crossfade ran its full length and showed
  the viewer nothing.
- **The camera pulsed at all ten section anchors**, including ones crossed in under a
  second, so it never settled.
- **Nothing responded to the pointer or to scroll speed.**
- **`SectionDepth` scaled and faded whole containers**, text included, so headings shrank
  and greyed out while they were being read.
- **Theme changes reshuffled the field** — colours were re-randomised per particle and the
  whole buffer re-uploaded, so switching theme scrambled the stars.

### Performance
- **Five simultaneous WebGL contexts**: the background field plus one R3F canvas per chapter
  transition, all rendering continuously whether on screen or not.
- **Six unreferenced modules** still in the tree, including the only consumers of
  `@react-three/drei`, `@react-three/postprocessing` and `postprocessing`.
- **A stale `dist/` was committed** despite being in `.gitignore`, carrying a 963 KB chunk
  built from source that no longer existed.

### Accessibility
- The guide pet had no Escape handler, no focus return, and no way to dismiss it.
- `FlowSteps` signalled done/active/queued **by colour alone**.
- Chapter transitions animated through framer-motion, bypassing the CSS reduced-motion block.
- `usePrefersReducedMotion` initialised to `false` and corrected in an effect, so the first
  render always assumed motion was allowed.

## What changed

| Area | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| WebGL contexts | 5 | 1 |
| Critical-path JS | 274 KB gzip | 140 KB gzip (three.js deferred behind first paint) |
| Scroll systems | 3 independent | 1 shared stage (`src/lib/stage.tsx`) |
| Particle formations | 5, with dead crossings | 7, with a test asserting no crossing is dead |
| Palettes | 2 conflicting | 1, four roles, all four themes drive everything |
| Résumé link | none | nav, hero and contact |

**New behaviour in the field:** the cursor opens a hole in it; scroll speed stretches and
brightens the motes so a flick leaves trails; it bursts as each act boundary crosses the
centre of the screen; far particles soften for atmospheric perspective; theme changes lerp
three uniforms instead of reshuffling the buffer; a frame-time watchdog sheds resolution and
half the particles on a device that cannot hold the frame.

**Section choreography** moved from container transforms to element-level staggering
(`src/components/Reveal.tsx`): eyebrow, rule, per-word heading wipe, body, then cards.
Containers now only drift ±24px, so nothing being read ever shrinks or fades.

**Three story diagrams** (`src/components/stories/`) replace the static mock dashboards for
the projects with no public URL — a replayable sequence with a numbered walkthrough that
highlights in time with it. Colour carries meaning throughout: sky for raw input, coral for
the system acting, amber for a person taking over, mint for confirmed.

## Second pass — what the first pass got wrong

Re-auditing after the first round turned up five problems, three of which the
first pass introduced.

- **Story labels were illegible on a phone.** SVG `<text>` scales with the
  viewBox. These cards render from ~320px on a phone to ~520px on a laptop
  against a 660-unit viewBox, so a 13-unit label arrived at 10px on desktop and
  **6.4px on mobile** — every label in all three diagrams was effectively
  invisible on a phone. Labels are now HTML positioned as a percentage of the
  frame (`DiagramLabel.tsx`), so they hold 11–13px at any card width, and long
  ones can wrap, which SVG text cannot do at all.
- **The theme switcher barely switched anything.** Completing the role tokens in
  the first pass gave all four themes the *same* amber secondary, and the same
  mint tertiary in three of four — so "change atmosphere" moved about half the
  page. Every theme now rotates all four roles.
- **Distinct accent tints had been flattened onto one.** A bulk colour replace
  in the first pass mapped eight separate light tints (`#ffe29a`, `#ffb29e`,
  `#b8f7dc`, …) onto a single primary-derived tint, so mint cards were labelled
  in coral. All 29 sites now derive their tint from the role they sit on.
- **Eleven hardcoded `rgba()` glows and washes ignored the theme entirely** —
  Leadership card fills and icon shadows, the SideWork wash, the hero visual
  glow, and two button shadows stayed coral/amber/mint in every theme.
- **A stray `<h2>` in the hero panel** sat between the `<h1>` and the first real
  section heading, so the document outline opened with a marketing line. It is
  a `<p>` now; the tablist already supplies the accessible name.

Contrast is now checked in **all four themes**, not just the default.

## Left undone, deliberately

- `index.html` still points `og:image` at a `/og.png` that does not exist, so shares render a
  blank card. A broken reference is slightly worse than no tag at all.
- `links.linkedin` and `links.github` are still empty strings and render nowhere.
- Three of the five projects still have no case study page, so their "read more" path ends
  at the story diagram.

## Verification

- `npm run build`, `npm run lint`, `npm test` (37 tests) all pass.
- Browser pass at 1440×900 and 390×844: exactly one canvas, zero horizontal overflow.
- Keyboard: skip link is the first stop, Enter opens the guide, Escape closes it, focus
  returns to the button that opened it.
- Reduced motion: the story diagrams render their finished frame and the full step list.
- Contrast: every informational text run, measured against its actual composited backdrop,
  meets its WCAG AA floor.
